The Grass Valley / Nevada City branch is where Nevada County members meet their neighbors and get to work. Meetings start with snacks and socializing, then move into the agenda: updates from working groups, branch business, and planning for upcoming actions and events.

New to DSA? A branch meeting is a great first step. Show up, say hi, and see what we're about. You don't need to be a member to attend.

Between meetings the branch hosts socials, tables at community events, and turns out for local government meetings when our campaigns are on the agenda.
