The Auburn branch brings together members from Auburn and the nearby foothill towns. The chapter itself was founded in Auburn in 2020, and the branch is the place to plug in locally: meet other members, hear what the chapter is working on, and help decide what we take on next.

Everyone is welcome at branch meetings, member or not.
