The Nevada County Sheriff's Office operates a network of Flock Safety automated license plate readers: cameras that photograph every passing vehicle, log the data, and share it across a national network of law enforcement agencies. The system was installed and operates without meaningful public oversight.

Our Deflock campaign is organizing to change that. We're pulling the records, building public awareness, and working toward getting these cameras out of our county.

The campaign meets the **fourth Thursday of every month**. New folks welcome. Bring questions, no background required.

**Get involved:** join `#deflock` on the chapter Slack or email [foothillsdsa@gmail.com](mailto:foothillsdsa@gmail.com). Grass Valley City Council meetings are on the calendar; come show your support and arrive by 5:45 PM if you'd like to give public comment.
