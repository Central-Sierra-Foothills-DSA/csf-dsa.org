Central Sierra Foothills DSA endorses the campaign, led by Nevada County for Palestine, for the Nevada County Board of Supervisors to adopt a Social and Ethical Investment Policy. Members approved the endorsement by chapter-wide ballot, with results announced on October 3, 2026.

**What the campaign is asking for.** The County Treasurer's investment policy currently weighs only safety, liquidity, and yield. The campaign seeks to add a social responsibility objective and screening criteria that would divest from, and prohibit investment in, entities that:

- finance, develop, or produce weapons;
- construct or operate private prisons; or
- commit or facilitate human rights abuses, including violations of international humanitarian law.

**Why it's winnable.** Alameda County adopted a comparable policy in September 2025, and the Nevada County Democratic Central Committee endorsed this campaign unanimously in May 2026. Nevada County is the first target. The same model can work in Placer, El Dorado, and Sierra counties.

**What our endorsement means.** We endorse the campaign and its goals, not any specific draft policy text, which will be revised through the Board's own process. Chapter members may speak in support before the Board of Supervisors when the proposal is on the agenda.
